# TASK 003 — Frozen price-confirmation pilot design

Baseline `cb0912f`, source `m2-systematic-10cycles-20261115-v2`. New run `m3-confirmation-10cycles-20261115-v1`. This design and config are written before execution of confirmation outcomes. No network client is instantiated: request budget zero. Only the existing ten exploration cycles and their 40 primary first-touch events are analyzed. Previous artifacts are byte-hashed for preservation, including holdout artifacts; holdout contents are not parsed or analyzed.

## Fixed risk set and clocks

The same 40 primary event IDs occur in C0/C1/C2, both condition-policy analyses and all strata. The proxy stratum has 25 initial events; the complementary stratum has 15. Both come from independent daily stratified SRS without replacement (12 of each stratum per cycle, 240 selected opportunities). Preserve original cycle/stratum N, n, n/N, N/n. No reweighting among confirmed events. Weighted ratios are descriptive event-risk-set summaries; no population inference, iid intervals or significance claim. Securities and cycles repeat and policy copies are dependent.

C0 signals at the original eligible +10% trade. C1 includes that trade as observation one, then requires at least three **distinct raw trade records** at/above the original fixed threshold spanning >=30 seconds. A later print can be the third or subsequent observation; signal only when that qualifying print is available. No timer-only signal, no retroactive price, no 60-second deadline for C1. Intervening below-threshold trades do not reset the count: this operational definition is counted persistence, not continuous support. Accumulation resets at the AH-to-next-PRE overnight gap; PRE/RTH are contiguous. C1 stops at cycle end. Known cancellations remove counted evidence once their notices arrive. Later cancellations do not erase an earlier signal; outcome censoring retains their effect. Original REST corrected-field reconstruction remains unchanged.

C2 operates on quote receipt timestamps, starts at first touch and ends at min(touch+60 seconds, cycle end). The valid preceding quote at touch may be the first observation, but its support clock starts at touch, not before. It needs distinct NBBO records supporting bid >= the original +10% threshold over >=10 seconds. Every intervening observed quote must stay valid/above threshold; below/invalid quotes or a freshness gap >5 seconds reset the support span. No forward-fill past five seconds to manufacture continuity. Bid/ask must be positive and uncrossed, displayed sizes positive, quote conditions regular (0/1). This is observed quote support, not independent proof of continuous consolidated-feed completeness. Only information at/before each candidate timestamp can qualify or flag it; future halt indications are not trigger inputs. No trade-at-ask requirement is added to C1/C0.

Missing/partial pagination or incomplete time bounds for C2 are `INSUFFICIENT_DATA`, separately from criterion-not-met `UNCONFIRMED`. A terminal provider window with no fresh valid observations is insufficient observation evidence, not a negative return. An ordinary quote gap is not silently converted into missing pagination. Existing overlapping complete quote windows may be merged, but gaps cannot be bridged. The original ±60-second windows need not cover later C1 entry or any 5–60-minute exit. No additional quotes are downloaded.

## Price and outcomes

C0/C1 market-observation bases are their actual signal trade prices. C2 uses a distinct quote signal timestamp and the latest eligible **as-of trade mark** at/before it, maximum age 60 seconds. Its observed-trade timestamp and source are separately stored. A synthetic anchor only tells the outcome engine the as-of basis at the quote signal; it is not reported as a new trade or executable entry. All pre-signal price extremes are excluded. NBBO ask at signal is independently retained as a quote-based entry mark; no interchange of quote ask and trade marks.

All policies retain initial events with no signal/missingness. Compute 5/15/30/60-minute wall-clock forward outcomes from each actual signal timestamp/price, plus session/cycle end, observed MFE/MAE, and +3/-3, +5/-3, +10/-5 first passage. Primary has no mechanical elapsed-trade-gap censoring; 300/900-second rules remain sensitivities. Missing pages, overnight, unknown evidence, invalidation and the 60-second mark-age policy still censor. These are gross eligible-trade-path observations. Barrier ties at one SIP timestamp remain ambiguous. First-touch and counted-evidence cancellations affect outcome validity when observed; no future cancel selects the risk set.

Entry-ask/exit-bid marks are computed only where both fresh valid preceding quotes exist within complete cached quote coverage and the price path is uncensored. They are explicitly quote-mark estimates, with no fills, order sizes or executable P&L. Missing returns are null, never zero.

## Comparisons and bias controls

For each policy, every horizon/barrier table partitions the **same initial denominator** into observed, censored confirmed, unconfirmed and insufficient data/mark buckets. Confirmation delay distributions and waiting-price changes are descriptions of confirmed observations. They must not replace the full-risk-set table with a survivor-only success rate. C0 outcomes remain available for all original events, including eventual failures to confirm. Record confirmation-by-5/15/30/60-minute initial-touch landmarks and C0 barriers occurring before delayed signals. Own-signal outcome clocks can end at different times; conditional paired-return tables are descriptive, not a causal strategy comparison, proof of protection during waiting, or a solution to immortal-time bias via delayed entry alone. No zero-return/no-position strategy is imputed for unconfirmed events.

AH/PRE/RTH summaries use initial-touch session; signal session is separately retained. The odd-lot sensitivity adds eligible subsequent observations after each original anchor while retaining all 40 initial events, original thresholds and C0 clocks. It does not restart at the 19 earlier odd-lot first touches or add the three odd-only opportunities from TASK 002.2. Thus it isolates follow-up evidence sensitivity on this fixed primary risk set; full odd-lot discovery sensitivity remains in the previous run. Preserve original price/quote flags, security identity, frozen float evidence and SEC context. No historical float approval, M1 change, threshold tuning, full 120-cycle ingestion or 40-cycle holdout analysis.

## Reproduction and integrity

`python -m market_lab.confirmation_pilot run` executes entirely offline and checkpoints each initial event in the new run directory. `validate` independently recomputes every signal/outcome from the existing normalized ticks and compares complete records, then checks raw source hashes, evidence timestamps, risk-set identity, original weights and protected artifacts. Code/config drift under the same run ID is rejected. Compact JSON retains every policy row and all gap outcomes; Parquet and a single validated `research_bundle.zip` remain local under ignored paths. Fresh checkout can read compact results; raw revalidation requires the immutable existing cache. No raw tick copies or credentials are committed.
