Missing a match means missing from the archived sources checked. October 26–27 have no raw-wire captures, so a missing Rule Match or Impact alert cannot establish that RTPR never sent it. October 28 begins at about 10:00 UTC. The quote-free price calculation uses fully post-publication one-minute trade bars through 20:00 ET and selects the high retrospectively. Public timing establishes order, not causation.
