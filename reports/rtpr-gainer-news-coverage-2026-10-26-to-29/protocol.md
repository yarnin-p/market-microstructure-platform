1. Start with all RTH Gainers in the dated US listing universe used for the series. Preserve the current threshold of at least +20% versus the comparable prior regular close and reconcile the stock-day count. Keep Extended Hours-only Gainers separate if a later report includes them.
2. Match issuer and historical ticker. Deduplicate copies of the same underlying event across providers and channels. Preserve article IDs and use the first independently evidenced publication time. Track same-day articles and earlier disclosures separately.
3. Compare each event with the RTPR full-article archive, raw or normalized Rule Match/Impact observations, and the delivered Telegram exports. Distinguish an article without an observed RTPR alert from an alert without an observed Telegram delivery. Search an independent news archive and SEC filings, then verify issuer and timing against the original release or filing. Keep third-party mentions, earlier announcements and filings with unresolved timing labeled in the audit. Record coverage and pagination. If an archive is absent, leave absence unresolved.
4. For a matched article or filing, use the first complete trade bar that starts after its known public time, then the highest trade price through 20:00 ET. Preserve source time and precision, baseline, peak, RTH gain, and whether the disclosure preceded the RTH peak. Keep the positive pre-peak cases in the daily addendum. Keep late and missing cases in the coverage audit. This measure describes observed price upside before costs.
5. Publish the daily linked section and machine-readable rows. Use the status vocabulary and columns in gainer_coverage.csv and matched_news_and_price.csv so counts remain comparable across dates.
