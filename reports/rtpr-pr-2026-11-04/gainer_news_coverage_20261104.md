## Population, price and evidence

- The Gainer screen uses the same prior-close band and listing scope as the prior date. November 3 regular close must be greater than $0 and at most $5, and November 4 regular-session high must be at least 20% above it. Listings use dated Nasdaq, NYSE American and NYSE common shares or common ADRs. The original dated universe and unadjusted Massive grouped bars were used to reconstruct the day. The reconstruction reproduced all 72 prior-day Gainers and resolved the reference close for November 4 candidates.
- The news slice is November 4 UTC. Price moves use the open of the first complete minute bar at or after the publication or SEC availability clock, through the highest subsequent bar before 20:00 ET (01:00 UTC the next day). The regular-session high comparison uses 09:30–16:00 ET. Filings use the SEC acceptance timestamp or the document directory time where noted. The price measurement starts after the chosen news clock. These are observed high-water marks. The study used trade aggregates and does not establish fills or causal attribution. The separate PR results use second bars near publication when available, so the four alert-covered Gainer returns in this audit can differ from those in that report.
- The original all-article archive ended at 09:39 UTC. Paginated RTPR per-ticker article retrieval covered all 61 Gainers for the full UTC date and yielded 21 article IDs before deduplication. The audit also used both Telegram exports, normalized RTPR releases and 150 archived wire frames. Continuous raw wire capture is unverified. The absence labels describe evidence in those sources.
- The independent screen read all four Massive News pages for November 4 (768 articles) and terminal pages for the screened SEC form families (1,598 filings, 24 matched to Gainers). Secondary recaps and ambiguous tags remain in the supplemental audit. These archives and form filters bound the news search.
